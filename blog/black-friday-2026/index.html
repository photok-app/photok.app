From Black Friday to Cyber Monday (November 27 to 30, 2026), Photok Pro is 50% off on Google Play. If you've been thinking about upgrading your private photo vault, this is the best time to do it.

What you get with Photok Pro

Photok already keeps your photos and videos encrypted and hidden on your phone. Photok Pro adds extra protection for when someone else gets their hands on your device:

  • Brute-force Protection: choose how many wrong password attempts are allowed. After that, Photok locks for 30 minutes or erases all files.
  • Panic Lock: lock Photok instantly by shaking your phone or turning it face down.
  • Intruder Warnings: Photok takes a photo with the front camera when someone enters a wrong password. These photos stay on your device.
  • All future Pro features at no extra cost.

Pay once, keep it forever

Photok Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Buy it during the sale and you keep it, including every Pro feature that comes later, for half the regular price.

How to get the deal

  1. Install or update Photok on Google Play.
  2. Open Photok and go to Photok Pro.
  3. The discounted price is shown in the app before you buy.

The offer ends on Cyber Monday, November 30. Have questions? Check the FAQ.
